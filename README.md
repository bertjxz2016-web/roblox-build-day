# Roblox Build Day

A responsive, single-page promotional website built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Open `dist/index.html` to view locally. No installation or build is required.

## Customize before announcing the event

All unconfirmed details are visible placeholders in `dist/index.html`. Replace the dates and times, location or online format, eligibility and age range, Studio experience requirements, cost, equipment and setup, supervision and accessibility arrangements, organizer/contact, deadline, registration URL, participation rewards and requirements, and winner prizes and judging criteria.

The five-day schedule and project ideas are explicitly proposed examples. Confirm them before changing their labels. Participation rewards and competition prizes are deliberately separate.

When registration opens, replace the disabled registration button with an anchor containing the confirmed registration URL, update its label to “Sign Up,” and revise the surrounding registration status text. Navigation and “Learn More” links currently lead to the relevant content within the page.

The illustration is original AI-generated artwork. No official Roblox sponsorship or affiliation is implied. Google Fonts are loaded remotely, with system font fallbacks. All other assets are local.

## Files

- `dist/index.html`: event content and semantic layout
- `dist/styles.css`: responsive design and reduced-motion preferences
- `dist/script.js`: accessible mobile menu interaction
- `dist/hero.png`: original game-world illustration
